# Dispatch Control Tower — Demo Pitch Plan v2 ("The 6 A.M. Problem")

The v1 demo was a feature tour: full-page screenshots with captions nobody
reads, no story, no numbers landing. v2 is a **directed film**: a problem →
turn → proof → close arc, a camera that points (spotlight + zoom), verbatim
voiceover copy, and every claim quantified from the seeded data.

**Target runtime:** ~3:30 · **Audience:** fleet owner / head dispatcher (5–50 trucks)
**Format:** Playwright-driven, self-playing, recorded to video; narration bar doubles as VO script.

---

## The arc

| Act | Time | Title | Job |
|-----|------|-------|-----|
| I   | 0:00–0:20 | THE 6 A.M. PROBLEM | Cold open on the pain — make them nod |
| II  | 0:20–1:10 | ONE SCREEN | Command: the board + the whole cockpit, pointed at piece by piece |
| III | 1:10–2:10 | 22 SECONDS TO DISPATCH | The turn: one dispatch end-to-end, timed |
| IV  | 2:10–2:35 | PROBLEMS FIND YOU | The safety net: late-risk + audit trail |
| V   | 2:35–3:15 | WHERE THE MONEY GOES | The owner's reason to buy |
| VI  | 3:15–3:30 | THE CLOSE | One-liner + CTA |

## Act I — THE 6 A.M. PROBLEM (cold open)

**On screen:** title card, then the board — uncovered loads with red chips,
Tyrone's red-haloed brick, Maria's STALE chip. No interaction yet.
**Camera:** slow spotlight sweep: backlog → late brick → stale chip.

> **VO:** "6 a.m. Five loads uncovered. One truck running late. A driver
> asking what's next. Yesterday, this was three phone calls, two
> spreadsheets, and a prayer. Today it's one screen."

**Proves:** we know their morning.

## Act II — ONE SCREEN (command)

Beat 1 — **KPI bar** (spotlight):
> "Your money, live. Revenue, margin, empty miles — not month-end
> accounting. Right now. And every number explains itself."
*(cursor hovers an ⓘ — the tooltip opens on camera)*

Beat 2 — **A lane** (spotlight Maria, zoom on HOS bar + STALE):
> "Every driver: legal hours as a fuel gauge. Booked time. Revenue. And when
> the clock data is three days old — the board says STALE. It never
> pretends to know more than it does."

Beat 3 — **The cockpit sweep** (rapid spotlight montage — each control gets
~2s: search box → equipment filter → the All/Rolling/Hazmat/Conflicts chips
(click "Rolling" on camera, board filters, click back) → zoom slider
(stretch the time axis on camera) → Day/3-day/**Week** flip → Today):
> "Up top, the cockpit. Search anything — a load, a city, a driver. Filter
> by trailer. One-click views: what's rolling, what's hazmat, what has
> conflicts. Stretch time in for precision, flip to the week for the big
> picture. It's your board — shape it."

Beat 4 — **The brick, decoded** (ZOOM one assigned brick: hatched deadhead
prefix → the red now-line → the ▶ ✓ ✕ lifecycle buttons):
> "Every brick tells its whole trip. The hatched lead-in? Empty miles to
> reach the pickup — you SEE waste before you accept it. The red line is
> right now. And the three little buttons run the trip: start it, deliver
> it, undo it."

Beat 5 — **The Yard** (spotlight the Yard panel):
> "Below the board, the Yard: every truck, trailer, and driver that's free
> at this exact minute — updated live as trips start and finish. What can I
> dispatch right now? That question answers itself."

Beat 6 — **Backlog** (spotlight the urgency chips):
> "And uncovered freight sorts itself by which pickup window burns first.
> Nobody keeps that list in their head anymore."

**Proves:** command without tribal knowledge; every glyph earns its pixels;
honesty as a feature.

## Act III — 22 SECONDS TO DISPATCH (the turn — start a visible timer)

Beat 1 — **The message** (Messages, Jake's thread, typed reply on camera):
> "Jake just emptied out in Kansas City. He wants miles. Watch how fast he
> gets them. — 'Got you covered.' Now let's mean it."

Beat 2 — **⚡Suggest** (spotlight ranking; ZOOM Dale's blocked row):
> "One click. The engine scores the whole fleet — empty miles, margin,
> legal hours, who's run this lane before. And Dale? Blocked. Eight hours
> short. That little ✗ is an FMCSA violation that never happened."

Beat 3 — **The commit** (modal; ZOOM the economics block):
> "Before anything commits: every check passed — and the load is priced.
> YOUR fuel burn. YOUR driver pay. You know the margin while the broker is
> still on the phone."

Beat 4 — **Landed** (brick appears; STAT CARD overlay):
> "Done. Brick on the lane. Hours booked. Jake's phone buzzing."
**Stat card:** `22 seconds · 0 phone calls · $50 margin locked · 0 empty-mile guesses`

Beat 5 — **The drag** (drag a second load onto a lane, drop-preview opens, cancel):
> "Prefer your hands on the board? Drag it. Same checks. Same pricing."

**Proves:** the core loop is faster AND safer than the phone.

## Act IV — PROBLEMS FIND YOU

Beat 1 — **Late risk** (spotlight strip; zoom the verdict):
> "Meanwhile, the board found a problem before you did. Tyrone is 444 miles
> from a window that closes in an hour — even a perfect run misses it. You
> get to call the broker first. That's the difference between an apology
> and a chargeback."

Beat 2 — **Alerts** (brief spotlight):
> "And every risk you accepted at dispatch is on the record. No surprises.
> No amnesia."

Beat 3 — **The compliance digest** (spotlight the board card; added it.44):
> "And it's not just today's trips. The board tracks the paperwork: an
> expired trailer registration, a medical certificate running out, a truck
> overdue for service — thirty days ahead, worst first."

Beat 4 — **The live block** (⚡Suggest on the Flatbed load — every driver ✗):
> "Try to cover that Flatbed load? Every single driver — blocked. Not
> because of the drivers: the only Flatbed in the yard has EXPIRED
> registration. One glance says 'fix the trailer', not 'find another
> driver'. That's a roadside out-of-service that never happened."

**Proves:** it watches when the dispatcher can't — trips AND paperwork.

## Act V — WHERE THE MONEY GOES

Beat 1 — **Money + live cost edit** (change diesel $4.00 → $4.60, SAVE on
camera; all-in $/mi updates; then ZOOM the red loser row):
> "This is where fleets bleed: freight that never paid. Diesel jumped this
> morning? Change it — every future dispatch reprices. And that red row?
> That load LOST $140. You found out the same week — not at tax time."

Beat 2 — **Analytics + settlements** (spotlight brokers, then settlements):
> "Which brokers actually pay. Which lanes repeat. Friday's driver
> settlements — one click, straight to CSV."

Beat 3 — **The headline number** (back to board; spotlight Empty-mi-saved; STAT CARD):
> "And the number that pays for the software: 395 empty miles avoided this
> week. At your dollar-sixty-seven a mile — that's $660 that stayed in the
> business."

**Proves:** ROI in their own units.

## Act VI — THE CLOSE

**On screen:** the board, wide. Title card over it.
> "One screen. Every truck. Every dollar. Every deadline. The problems find
> you — before they find your customers. **Dispatch Control Tower.**"
**End card:** logo line + "Ask for the demo login — run it on your own freight."

## Cinematic toolkit (new in pitch.mjs v2)

| Tool | Implementation |
|------|----------------|
| **Title cards** | Full-screen overlay div, act number + title, 2.5s fade |
| **Spotlight** | Overlay div positioned over the target's bounding box with a huge `box-shadow: 0 0 0 9999px rgba(0,0,0,.55)` + blue ring; everything else dims |
| **Zoom** | `#app` gets `transform: scale(1.6)` with `transform-origin` at the target's center, 600ms ease; zoom out before navigation |
| **Ghost cursor** | Injected dot that trails the Playwright mouse so viewers see every click |
| **Stat cards** | Centered overlay card with 3–4 big numbers, amber accent, 4s hold |
| **Timer** | Small corner stopwatch overlay started at Act III beat 1, stopped on commit — the "22 seconds" is real, on camera |
| **VO export** | The script writes every narration line to `demo/narration.md` — ready for a recorded human/AI voiceover pass |

## Seed upgrades (numbers that land)

- **Richer history:** ~16 delivered loads over 21 days (not 5 over 11) across
  6 lanes and 4 brokers — weekly revenue lands ~$18–24k for a 5-truck fleet,
  which reads as real. Two deliberate money-losers for the red rows.
- **Realistic rates:** $1.90–$2.90 per loaded mile by lane; savedMi 8–35 per load.
- **The math must add up on camera:** Empty-mi-saved × all-in $/mi is quoted
  in the VO — seed so the quoted figure matches what the KPI bar shows.
- Same rerunnability guarantee (prior-org purge) as today.
